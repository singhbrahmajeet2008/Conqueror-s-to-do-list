# ⚔️ IMPERIVM — The Conqueror's War Ledger
### *“He who defends everything defends nothing.”*

**IMPERIVM** is an interactive, historical conqueror-themed task management system and campaign ledger inspired by history's greatest military commanders: **Napoleon Bonaparte**, **Alexander the Great**, **Julius Caesar**, **Hannibal Barca**, and **Genghis Khan**.

---

## 🏛️ What Makes IMPERIVM Unique

Unlike generic to-do apps, IMPERIVM transforms mundane daily tasks into high-stakes military campaign directives:

1. **5 Legendary Commander Allegiances**:
   - **Napoleon Bonaparte** (*La Grande Armée*): French imperial navy & gold aesthetic, bee emblems, Eagle crests.
   - **Alexander the Great** (*Macedonian Empire*): Royal Tyrian purple & gold bronze, Vergina Sun heraldry.
   - **Julius Caesar** (*Legions of the SPQR*): Classical Roman crimson, Carrara marble & Aquila gold.
   - **Hannibal Barca** (*The Punic Legions*): Alpine terracotta, bronze shields & war elephant motifs.
   - **Genghis Khan** (*The Golden Horde*): Steppe azure sky, tungsten iron & wolf/falcon badges.

2. **Interactive Conquest Map (The Known World)**:
   - 8 historical provinces (Gaul, Germania, Italia, Hellas, Anatolia, Persia, Hispania, Aegyptus).
   - As you conquer directives (complete tasks), provinces switch from contested fog to annexed territory, raising your imperial morale and advancing your military rank from *Centurion* up to *Supreme Imperator*.

3. **Tactical Focus Siege (Integrated Battle Pomodoro Clock)**:
   - Built directly into every directive: launch a 25m or 50m focused assault with cadence of war drums and Roman numeral timer dial.
   - Proclaiming victory automatically marks the objective as annexed with a fanfare.

4. **Synthesized Web Audio Engine**:
   - Zero external audio dependencies (works 100% offline).
   - Procedural sounds:
     - ⚔️ Steel blade clash upon task conquest
     - 🎺 Royal brass trumpet fanfare upon territory annexation
     - 🥁 Deep war drum cadence
     - 📜 Quill scribing sound on directive decree
     - 🔥 Ambient war council campfire crackle

5. **Historical Campaign Presets (1-Click Deployment)**:
   - **Napoleon's Austerlitz Master Plan (1805)**
   - **Alexander's Gaugamela Blueprint (331 B.C.)**
   - **Caesar's Double Circumvallation at Alesia (52 B.C.)**
   - **Hannibal's Crossing of the Alps (218 B.C.)**

6. **Comprehensive Task Management Features**:
   - Priority rankings: *Decisive Campaign (Urgent)*, *Major Offensive (High)*, *Standard Manoeuvre (Medium)*, *Skirmish (Low)*.
   - Subtasks / *Tactical Milestones* with progress bar percentage.
   - War Fronts (Categories): Imperial Governance, Grand Academy, Military Drill, War Treasury, Camp Life + Custom War Fronts.
   - Hour of Engagement (due dates & times with overdue alerts).
   - Strategic intel & notes with tags (#Cavalry, #Logistics, etc.).
   - LocalStorage auto-save with JSON export & restore.
   - Full keyboard shortcuts (`N` for new directive, `S` for siege clock, `1-5` for commanders, `/` for search).
